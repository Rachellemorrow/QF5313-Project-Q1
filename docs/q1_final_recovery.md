## Permitted conventions versus missing evidence

The examination explicitly permits **q=0 with justification** and interpolation
of SHIBOR to option tenors. Zero dividends are an approximation, not fabrication;
students can approve them while disclosing dividend sensitivity. Same-date closes
and volume>=1 with missing-spread disclosure are feasible existing conventions.
Neither historical bid/ask nor OI is an indispensable input to BS.

The existing rate adapter supports explicit continuous-zero interpretation or
simple ACT/360 deposit conversion, then interpolated continuous zeros. Choosing
an approximation, ACT/365F expiry times, tenor coordinates and a fixing/closing
alignment policy requires a documented student decision. The current approval
reference/node-day gates are conservative governance choices, not proof that
SHIBOR data are missing. No convention is silently approved here because it cannot
remove the independent metadata and strike-support blockers.

External verification still required: effective-dated ID→underlying/C-P/strike/
exact expiry/unit mapping, adjustment history or authoritative evidence of no
change for the sampled interval, quote premium and volume units, and reuse rights.
Listing/expiry dates alone do not establish strike/unit effective intervals.
The UTC retrieval time of an undated current master is not an asserted historical
validity interval. The old SSE ID needs all missing contract attributes. Index
records additionally need matching dated index spot and full contract specs.

one expiry, >=5 distinct liquid strikes with C/P pairs (>=10 quotes), contemporaneous
unadjusted spot, exact contract attributes/units, all required SHIBOR nodes and
explicit q/rate conventions. Obtain a small authorized matched export rather than
more single-contract histories. Five strikes is the implementation minimum, not
assurance of stable five-parameter estimation; wider support is preferable.
BS/parity and matched SciPy/Torch SVI could then run; neural, calendar and missing-
quote experiments may remain unsupported. A useful maturity study needs multiple
well-supported expiries and enough observations for disjoint validation/evaluation.

**Full historical coverage is not achievable from the currently saved evidence,
and is not demonstrated achievable within the bounded public-source checks.**
